Built for how a barangay actually works

Three rules sit behind every screen.

Bills come from photos

Every reading is saved with a meter photo and location, so a bill can always be checked against what the meter showed.

Balances update on posting

When the cashier posts a payment, the balance changes and a numbered receipt prints. Nothing waits for end-of-month tallying.

Every change is logged

Corrections, rate changes and payments are written to an audit log. The system backs up daily.

From meter to receipt

One monthly cycle, four steps.

  1. Read

    Meter readers photograph each meter and enter the reading. It works offline and syncs when there is signal.

  2. Review

    Unusually high readings are flagged for a second look before any bill is made.

  3. Bill

    The system applies the rate brackets and releases bills once all meters are read and flags are cleared.

  4. Pay

    Residents see the bill in the app or by SMS and pay at the barangay hall. The cashier posts it and prints a receipt.

For residents

A phone app that shows what you owe and why. Residents without a smartphone get bill notices and due-date reminders by SMS.

For meter readers

A route list, a camera, and a sync button. Readings are saved on the phone first, so a weak signal never blocks the job.

For barangay staff

A web dashboard for the admin and the cashier. What each person sees depends on their role.
